   invitation on the capture is a Page's newsletter invitation: no member link

WHAT EACH DOES, and what it refuses:

* THE ADDRESS IS A CONSTANT: one of the two tabs `invitation_manager` admits
  (lane A, READ-PAST-AN-ACT-SUBSTRING), whose one forbidden substring,
  `invitation`, each spec excuses by equality at the write door. The member
  never enters a navigation (PAGE-MAY-VETO-NEVER-SUPPLY).
* THE TARGET IS HIS LINK: a new target kind, `invitation_profile`, parsed by
  the named-profile parser -- a profile link or a member id, never a name,
  refused before a token exists -- and canonical as the link, exactly as
  `rail_profile` is. Nothing loads it.
* THE AIM IS THE ROW'S OWN LINKS, IN THE PAGE: the control wearing LinkedIn's
  whole word, whose nearest `role=listitem` is an invitation of its tab's
  measured shape (received: exactly two buttons, `Ignore` and `Accept`, and a
  line ending `wants to connect` holding the member's link; Sent: exactly one
  `Withdraw` link), linking the member he named and no other member. XPath 1.0,
  evaluated by the browser, built from constants and the segment of his link
  (refused unless letters, digits, hyphens, underscores or percent octets),
  with `dom`'s own segment expression imported. What crosses back is a COUNT.
  No label is read: every control's aria-label carries the member's name live.
* EXACTLY ONE such row, or no press: none reads not pending (answered, below
  the first render, or another member), two read unknown (choosing by
  position is refused).
* ONE STRICT PRESS through `perform`'s one click site, and nothing appended:
  `readonly.SANCTIONED_MUTATIONS` is unchanged at sixteen.
* AFTER THE PRESS, before anything navigates, `read_after_press` counts the
  visible dialogs, their buttons by a closed list of LinkedIn's words, whether
  the address moved (the Withdraw link's address is the site root) and whether
  the row is still drawn -- and decides nothing. Nothing in a dialog is pressed.
* A FRESH RENDER OF THE TAB DECIDES, `group_leave`'s rule: the member's row
  still drawn is NOT performed; no row carrying the member AND exactly one
  invitation row fewer than the preview counted is performed; anything else
  is unknown.
