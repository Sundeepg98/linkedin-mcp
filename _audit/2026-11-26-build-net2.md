## 5. N 200 -- hide an ad he names: NOT BUILT, the aim has no source

THE RULING AD-HIDE-AT-HIS-NAMED-AD (the answer to `Q-AD-HIDE-TARGET`, applied
by readers-net26's round 2) builds the hide "at an ad he names: an urn a
reader returned to him that he hands back. The tool never picks an ad."
Measured on this tree, that sentence has no reader to stand on.

* NO SHIPPED READER RETURNS AN AD'S URN. The readers of item keys are
  `linkedin_my_activity_items` (the posts on his own profile that he wrote)
  and `linkedin_read_named_profile`'s `activity_items` (the posts of a member
  he names); neither draws an ad. `linkedin_search_content` returns no link
  at all (its cards draw no permalink), `linkedin_surface_census` substitutes
  urns out before it counts, and `/feed/` carries zero item permalinks (the
  first tool's own docstring). So no ad can be "an urn a reader returned to
  him".
* ON THE CAPTURE, THE AD'S URN IS INSIDE ITS OWN MENU. The only place session
  11's ad draws its urn is its menu's Embed entry (readers-net26), which exists
  only after the ad's trigger is pressed, on `/feed/`, where the ad is
  LinkedIn's choice. Opening item menus to find the ad he named would be
  pressing items the page chose (PAGE-MAY-VETO-NEVER-SUPPLY).
* NOR CAN A SHIPPED READ OPEN THAT MENU. A trigger never opened carries no
  `aria-expanded` (2 of 2 on the closed feed capture, 1 of 1 on the closed
  permalink; `aria-expanded="true"` only once pressed), so neither of
  `press.py`'s shapes matches it. Session 11 opened the ad's as a capture
  press. A write's own press, through `perform`, can open one, as `N 154`'s
  does -- but a write's preview may not press, so it cannot read the urn first.
* AN AD'S OWN PERMALINK IS UNMEASURED. `N 154`'s route, the urn's own
  `/feed/update/<urn>/` page, was loaded for a member's post (load 29) and
  never for an ad, so whether an ad's permalink draws its control menu with
  `Hide this ad` is unseen.
