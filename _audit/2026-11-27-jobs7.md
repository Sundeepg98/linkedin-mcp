## 2. The instrument: session 12's keyed skeletons, read by keyed shape

**The method is the shipped one** (`scripts/_build_live11_word_skeletons.py`,
`scripts/_build_jobs2_word_skeletons.py`): a closed candidate word is hashed with the session's key
by `_probe_live_lane_session_2.value_shape` and compared, as a whole text or a whole attribute
value, with the keyed skeleton. A match is a MEASURED word; nothing assumed is ever written. Lane
jobs7's scratch probes (`kprobe.py`, `kstruct.py`, `khref.py`, `kchip.py`; disposable, section 9)
print only the candidate words they were given and integer counts -- never a hash, the key or a
page value.

**Controls, both ways, on every probe run:**
- PROVENANCE: `tracked_copy(keyed skeleton)` equals the committed tracked copy, byte for byte, for
  every capture probed (`provenance: ... True` on all nine).
- NEGATIVE: the same candidates under a RANDOM key match nothing (0 hits of 3180 candidates on each
  AI-search load).
