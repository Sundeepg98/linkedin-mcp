## 4. The badge bracket, and this lane's call on the received tab

`readonly.py`'s admission of the three `/mynetwork/` lists says the
pending-invitation badge a load may clear is a passive cost, "bracketed by the
reader as `linkedin_connections` brackets it". `badge_bracket.py` is that
bracket's verdict: the badge read on the feed before (unreadable: nothing else
loads), the list, the badge read again on the list's own nav (measured: the
network control there reads `Network, 0 new notifications`, so no third load is
needed). On the SENT TAB and the FOLLOWING LIST a badge unreadable after, or
moved, WITHHOLDS the list -- `linkedin_connections`' rule. On the RECEIVED TAB
it does not: that page IS the invitations the badge counts, and
RECEIVED-INVITATIONS-READ-STAYS-ADMITTED keeps the read admitted with whatever
it changes in their seen state as a passive cost, recorded and unmeasured. So a
badge that fell there is that cost MEASURED, returned beside the list. Put to
the orchestrator in section 9 and CONFIRMED in round 2,
RECEIVED-BADGE-IS-THE-READ'S-COST: reading the tab inherently clears the pending
badge, on the `linkedin_notifications` precedent; the tool's docstring now says
so before anything else.
