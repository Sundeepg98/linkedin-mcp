## 3. AND THE LOAD DID NOT SETTLE IT. THREE MEASURED REASONS, NOT ONE OPINION

This is the part that changes what the operator is being asked, so each reason
cites the reading it comes from rather than an inference.

**1. THE NUMBER HAS NO ESTABLISHED CAPTION.** Both metric labels came back
`entity_linked: "unwalked"` and therefore `label_shape: "<redacted>"` -- the
reader's ancestor walk ran out of its six hops before it could establish the
row carries no member link, and anything but a flat `no` costs the label. So
**108 is a real number whose unit and period are not established.** A before
value you cannot caption cannot be compared with an after value.

That is the conservative branch working as designed, and the load record says
so and says the remedy: a capture of the real DOM, not a bigger hop budget --
raising the budget makes the walk reach `main` and answer `yes` everywhere,
which redacts more rather than less.

**2. WHICH CLASS OF SEARCH FEEDS THE COUNTER IS OPEN.** The load established
that search-as-a-category is tracked and attributed. Whether an ordinary
member search, a Recruiter search and a logged-out search all feed the same
counter is not answered -- the load record files it as open and notes the
burden sits on the addition.

**3. AND THE AFTER HALF REQUIRES THE ACT THE RULING GATES.** A baseline is one
end of a measurement. The other end is the counter read again AFTER a people
search has been performed -- and performing one means opening
`/search/results/people/`, which is precisely the address this ruling exists
to decide.

> **The evidence path the consent brief recommends is CIRCULAR: the
> measurement that would justify opening the surface can only be completed by
> opening it.**

Not a defect in that brief -- it named LOAD A correctly and LOAD A was worth
taking for its own sake. What it could not know is that the instrument would
come back unable to caption its own number.

Stated so the next session starts from evidence rather than re-deriving this:

* **(a) RULE WITH THE EMISSION UNMEASURED.** The consent brief's option 1,

  rendered row is the ceiling, and `linkedin_who_viewed_me` is admitted on

* **(b) AUTHORISE ONE SEARCH AS AN EXPERIMENT.** The only route to the AFTER

  experiment IS the act under question. **Its precondition is a capture of the
  analytics DOM** -- without a caption on the counter, an experiment returns a

  ruling stands: *the boundary decides what may be OPENED, the shaper decides
  what may be SAID.* Prongs (a) and (b) are about opening. Nineteen reads on a
  page full of other people's names are a shaper question that can be answered
  in parallel and does not wait on either.

**What this wave does NOT recommend.** Nothing. The blocker is queued DECIDE
and this document decides nothing; it removes one wrong premise and prices the
