### 2.2 What is built: one module, five writes, two declared mutations

`linkedin_server/conversation_menu.py` (new) holds the selectors, readings and
verdicts; `writes` holds the five specs and delegates, as it does to
`group_leave`.

* THE PREVIEW PRESS, `conversation_menu.preview_press`. Refuses before any press
  unless the page draws EXACTLY ONE row wearing the active marker, holding
  EXACTLY ONE trigger reading collapsed, with no item drawn. Then: the trigger
  pressed (`_open`, one strict `click`); a bounded wait for items, deciding
  nothing; every word of a closed vocabulary counted INSIDE THE PAGE by
  Playwright's role engine (an item no word names is counted, never read);
  Escape (`_close`, one `press`); a bounded wait and a read that the menu is
  closed again. Nothing inside the menu is ever pressed by it. A menu left open
  makes every verdict `unknown`. These two calls are the module's only mutating
  call sites: `readonly.SANCTIONED_MUTATIONS` entries 17 and 18, argued beside
  them on the ruling's words.
* THE PREVIEW (`writes.observe`, `state_from` `conversation_menu`): the RECEIPT
  GUARD first -- `/messaging/compose/` read by `threads.read_conversation_list`
  and refused while any rendered row reads unread (`threads.receipt_guard`,
  `allow_unread` False; the landing reaches it only as a comparison's
  boolean) -- then the conversation he named, by its thread id, the landing
  VETOING by raising; the thread's counts; the preview press. Two loads.
* THE CONFIRMED PRESS (`writes.perform`): gate 5 reads the TRIGGER, collapsed,
  in the one active row (`click_from_state` `menu_closed` -- the word is not
  readable without opening the menu); click 1 opens it through `perform`'s one
  click site; `conversation_menu.menu_item_gate` RE-READS THE WORD inside the
  menu that opened and appends the item -- strict, visible-only, by its exact
  name -- only when the act's word is drawn exactly once and its inverse not at
  all. Otherwise the same trigger is appended to CLOSE the menu, and nothing in
  it is pressed. Click 2 is the item, or that close. What the item press drew
  is read before anything else (`read_after_press`: dialogs, their buttons
  against a closed vocabulary, counted).
* THE VERIFICATION (`writes._verify_after`): the same preview press on a FRESH
  load of the conversation's address (mute, unmute, archive) or IN PLACE with
  no new load (the unread mark -- a load is believed to mark it read). An item
  the gate refused was never pressed: `not_changed_by_this_call`, the specs'
  `not_performed_state`, so a conversation ALREADY muted is never reported
  muted by this call. Archive's positive is DERIVED and says so: a fresh render
  drawing rows and none wearing the active marker.
* THE DELETE is RECEIPT-ONLY (`J-163-RECEIPT-ONLY-WRITE`'s class): it declares
  its outcome unverifiable (`Unverifiable`), presses `Delete conversation`
  after re-reading it, counts what the press drew, and presses nothing else --
  a confirmation is reported and never answered.
* THE PREVIEW NAMES WHOSE CONVERSATION IT IS (`PREVIEW-NAMES-THE-RESOLVED-
  TARGET`): `threads.name_the_reply_recipient` now serves these five acts
  under the key `whose_conversation`, in a new dict after the grant's copy was
