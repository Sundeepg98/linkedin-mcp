## 5. METHOD

**One address per read row, from the strongest committed source**, in the
bucket-3 order: a shipped code symbol; a committed fixture or probe literal;
the row's own census cell; a prior audit. When sources name different pages,
the one that draws the READ payload wins and the others are driven too, in
`also_driven`. When the candidates disagree and nothing committed says which
draws the payload, the row is NEEDS-SESSION -- `J 124`, whose candidates are the
admitted posting's Premium panel and the refused Insights tab.

**Two widenings of the source column, both needed by real rows**: a committed
FIXTURE may be cited (`.html`), because an address drawn on a capture is
recorded nowhere else, and a `::token` may carry a hyphen, because the route a
fixture draws is spelled with one.

**Placeholders**: `placeholder-org`, `placeholder`, `1234567890`, `12345`. The
patterns accept a SHAPE, not a value, so no verdict depends on the value.

**The boundary is imported, never re-read, and so is the bucket-3 checker.**
`scripts/check_jobs_directions.py` calls `check_read_addresses`'
`shape_problems`, `boundary_problems`, `control_problems` and `refusal_of` on
the read rows unchanged.

**Captures read, all under the main checkout's gitignored `_state/`**: the
`/jobs/alerts/` landing (the jobs home), the recommended collection, the company
root, and the live job-detail readings. Only counts and shapes were printed;
nothing from them is in a tracked file except through the two builders, whose
docstrings say what each carries over and invents.
